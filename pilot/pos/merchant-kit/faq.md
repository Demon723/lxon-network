# Helios POS — Merchant FAQ

## What is this?

It's a card terminal that works even when the phone network is down.
It uses satellites instead of cell towers to settle payments.

## Do I need a bank account?

No. Settlement goes to your M-Pesa or bank account — your choice.

## How long does settlement take?

About 30 minutes. Much faster than waiting for end-of-day batch.

## What if it stops working?

Call this number: +254-XXX-XXXX. We replace the device free within
24 hours.

## What about the solar panel?

It charges the battery in daylight. One day of sun = one day of use.
Bring it inside at night to charge via the cable.

## Can I use my existing card terminal?

Yes — keep using it. Helios is for when the network is down, and for
customers who don't have M-Pesa.

## Who owns the device?

We do. You're borrowing it for the pilot. Return it if you want to stop.
