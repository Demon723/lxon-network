# Staff Training

## Goal

By end of day 1, every merchant staff member can:
1. Accept a payment
2. View the balance
3. Request support
4. Handle a failed transaction

## Training script (3 hours)

### Hour 1 — Introduction (15 min)

"Thank you for trying the Helios terminal. This is a satellite POS — it works
where the phone network doesn't. You don't need a bank account. Settlement
happens in 30 minutes."

Show the device. Let them hold it. Point out:
- Solar panel on the back (charges in daylight)
- NFC reader on the front (tap here)
- Screen shows the amount
- Receipt prints from the side

### Hour 1 — Core flow (45 min)

Walk through a real transaction:

1. Customer taps card / phone on the NFC reader
2. Terminal shows "Processing…"
3. Terminal prints a receipt
4. Terminal shows "Settled — $X.XX"

Practice 5 transactions with a test card.

### Hour 1 — Troubleshooting (30 min)

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| "No response" | Press the power button for 3 sec to wake |
| "Satellite searching…" | Wait 60 sec — do not tap again |
| "Payment failed" | Ask customer to try again / use another card |
| "Out of paper" | Open side door, reload roll |
| "Device hot" | Normal — it's charging from solar |

### Hour 2 — Balance & reporting (30 min)

- Show how to view today's balance (menu → Reports → Today)
- Show how to view pending settlements
- Show how to export to M-Pesa (menu → Settlement → M-Pesa)

### Hour 2 — Support flow (30 min)

- Call this number: +254-XXX-XXXX (LXON support)
- We answer within 5 minutes during business hours
- If no answer: WhatsApp the photo of the screen

### Hour 3 — Practice + quiz (30 min)

Run 10 practice transactions. Ask staff to teach a colleague.

## Post-training checklist

- [ ] Device installed and charging
- [ ] Test card works
- [ ] Receipt paper loaded
- [ ] Staff can take a payment unsupervised
- [ ] Cheat sheet posted behind the counter
- [ ] Support number saved in 3 staff phones

## Daily check-in script (15 min, day 2)

"How many transactions today? Any failures? Anything confusing?"
Log answers in `metrics.md`.
