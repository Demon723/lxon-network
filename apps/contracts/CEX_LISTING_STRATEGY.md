## 💡 Alternative: CEX Custodial Service

**Some CEXs offer custodial listing:**

**How it works:**
- CEX holds your tokens in their wallet
- Users can trade on the CEX
- CEX handles all blockchain operations
- No need for direct deposits

**Benefits:**
- Faster approval
- Lower requirements
- CEX handles technical integration
- Better user experience

**Drawbacks:**
- CEX custody (not your keys)
- Less decentralization
- Withdrawal limits
- Higher fees

**Exchanges with custodial options:**
- Binance (for some tokens)
- OKX (custodial listing)
- Huobi (custodial listing)
