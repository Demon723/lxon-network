# 60-Second Pilot Video Script

**Shot 1 (5s)** — Establishing shot. Sunrise over Nairobi skyline. Text:
"LXON. Payments where the network ends."

**Shot 2 (10s)** — B-roll of a dirt road, then a small market stall. A
customer taps their phone on the Helios terminal. The terminal prints a
receipt.

**Voiceover**: "In rural Kenya, the mobile network stops. But payments
don't have to."

**Shot 3 (10s)** — Close-up of the terminal's solar panel in sunlight.
Then the Iridium antenna.

**Voiceover**: "Helios is a satellite POS. It uses Iridium to settle
on-chain — in under 30 minutes. No bank account. No cell tower."

**Shot 4 (10s)** — Merchant (real person, smiling) counting cash at the
end of the day. Then a phone showing M-Pesa settlement notification.

**Voiceover**: "This coffee shop used to lose sales when M-Pesa went down.
Now they never miss a transaction."

**Shot 5 (10s)** — Quick cuts: 3 different merchants, 3 different locations.
Each taps a card. Each prints a receipt.

**Voiceover**: "10 terminals. 3 merchants. 100 transactions in 4 weeks.
Post-quantum security. Satellite settlement. Real money."

**Shot 6 (10s)** — Final shot: the LXON logo on the terminal screen, then
on a card. Text: "LXON. The Layer-1 for the edge of coverage."
URL: lxon.example

**Shot 7 (5s)** — Outro. "Pilot report coming week 8."

**Total: 60 seconds**

## Production notes

- Shoot on a real 2026 smartphone. No stabilization — authentic.
- Merchant consent form signed before filming.
- Do NOT stage transactions. Capture real ones.
- Audio: record merchant soundbites in situ (market noise is fine).
- Color grade: warm, documentary look.
- Deliver: 60s cut + 30s cut + 15s cut + 5s bumper.
