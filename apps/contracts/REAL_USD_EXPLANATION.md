## Options to Get Real USD

### Option 1: Convert via Centralized Exchange (CEX) ⭐ *Most Practical*

**Steps:**
1. Transfer WETH from LXON to Ethereum Mainnet (if bridge exists)
2. Send WETH to a CEX (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, etc.)
3. Convert WETH to USDC/USDT on the CEX
4. Withdraw USDC/USDT to your bank account

**Issues:**
- Need a bridge from LXON to Ethereum
- Need CEX account with KYC
- Takes time for withdrawals

### Option 2: Bridge USDC from Ethereum to LXON
