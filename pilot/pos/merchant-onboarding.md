# Merchant Onboarding

## Target profile

- 20-200 daily transactions
- Sells to end consumers
- Owner-operated
- Located in target region
- Already accepts mobile money

## Recruitment funnel

1. **Lead list** (target: 30 merchants)
   - Walk the market districts with a local guide
   - Ask existing mobile-money agents for referrals
   - Post in local business WhatsApp groups

2. **Initial pitch** (15 min, in person)
   - Show the device. Let them hold it.
   - Key line: "This works where the phone network doesn't."
   - Key line: "No bank account needed. Settlement in 30 min."
   - Key line: "Zero fees for the first 90 days."
   - Handout: `merchant-kit/pitch-one-pager.pdf`

3. **Terms** (target: 10 signed)
   - **Free hardware** (we retain ownership)
   - **0% transaction fee** for 90 days, then 0.5%
   - **We provide**: device, SIM, solar panel, training, support
   - **They provide**: counter space, ~2h training, weekly feedback call

4. **Onboarding (day 1, 3 hours)**
   - Install device + solar panel
   - Register merchant wallet
   - Load 1,000 LXON test credits
   - Train on 3 core flows: accept payment, view balance, request support
   - Run 5 practice transactions
   - Give printed cheat sheet: `merchant-kit/cheat-sheet.pdf`

5. **Follow-up (days 2, 7, 14, 28)**
   - Day 2: 30-min visit, resolve any blockers
   - Day 7: 15-min call, collect first-week metrics
   - Day 14: 30-min visit, tune
   - Day 28: 60-min interview, video capture

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already use M-Pesa" | "Keep using it. This is for when the network is down, and for customers who don't have M-Pesa." |
| "Is this a scam?" | "We're not asking for money. We give you the device. You keep the transactions." |
| "How do I get my money?" | "Daily settlement to your M-Pesa or bank account. We handle the conversion." |
| "What if it breaks?" | "We replace it free within 24 hours. Call this number." |
| "The government will ask questions" | "We're registered and compliant. Here's our paperwork." |

## Contract (1-page, plain language)

This agreement ("Pilot Agreement") is entered into between LXON Labs ("we")
and the merchant ("you").

1. **Hardware loan.** We lend you one Helios POS terminal and solar panel. You
   keep it safe and return it on request. It remains our property.

2. **No fees.** Transaction fees are 0% for the first 90 days, then 0.5% per
   transaction.

3. **Settlement.** We settle net proceeds daily to your M-Pesa or bank
   account. You bear no FX risk.

4. **Support.** We provide phone support during local business hours. We
   replace broken hardware within 24 hours.

5. **Data.** You consent to collection of transaction metrics for pilot
   improvement. No personally identifiable data is shared externally.

6. **Termination.** Either party may terminate with 7 days' written notice.
   You return the hardware upon termination.
